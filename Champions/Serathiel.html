Serathiel

Serathiel

Jun 16, 20263 min read

  • Marksman
  • Celestial-Concord
  • Executioner

Serathiel is a disciplined, immaculate archer descended from The Celestial Concord’s judgment-focused sect — the philosophical inheritance left behind when Asterion’s warnings were dismissed and the order fractured. Where Lumia inherited the Concord’s instinct to preserve and guide, Serathiel inherited its instinct to judge. She fights with a serene, unnerving calm, as though every battle were simply a verdict being delivered on schedule.

“Compassion is admirable… yet too often it blinds,” she has told Lumia. Lumia’s reply — “Judgment without kindness is mere cruelty” — captures the divide between them: two ends of the same spectrum, neither fully wrong.

The Hunt for Varesa

Serathiel considers Varesa an aberration, something that by the Concord’s old doctrines should not exist, and has hunted her before. Their clashes are said to light the horizon in black and gold. “You should not exist,” Serathiel tells her. “And yet — here I stand,” Varesa replies.

Views on Other Champions

  • CR-9 intrigues her — a machine incapable of sin, and therefore, in her eyes, outside judgment entirely.
  • Ka’Ruun’s volatile star-soul reminds her of fallen Concord apprentices.
  • Garvak she despises outright: a war-god worshipper who believes strength alone proves righteousness. “Judgment is earned, not claimed,” she tells him.

The Vigil

Serathiel maintains a vigil at the edge of The Starfall Crater, near the exposed foundations of Celestial Concord HQ. She is convinced something moved in the fog near those foundations, unaligned with the vertical, and refuses to stand down despite Asterion’s belief that she is guarding a memory rather than a threat.

Abilities (Overview)

  • Passive — Edict of Radiance: Enemies hit by abilities or three consecutive autos become Judged, taking bonus damage and granting Serathiel attack speed and bonus Light Shards on takedown.
  • Q — Dawnbreak Shot: A narrow beam-arrow that applies Judged and deals increased damage to already-Judged targets.
  • W — Ritual of Poise: Empowers her next attack into a longer-range, bonus-damage strike that crits Judged targets.
  • E — Crescent Rivenstep: A crescent sweep that slows, with stronger effects against Judged enemies at max range.
  • R — Final Dawn: Ascended Verdict: A two-phase ultimate — first marks a target as Condemned and can execute them with a javelin, then ascends into an empowered state with piercing shots and doubled Judged effects.

Connections

  • The Celestial Concord — her order’s judgment-focused lineage.
  • Lumia — philosophical counterpart from the preservation-focused sect.
  • Varesa — hunted as an aberration.
  • Celestial Concord HQ and The Starfall Crater — site of her ongoing vigil.
  • Asterion — has tried, unsuccessfully, to convince her to end the vigil.
